# The Last Pot of Lemon Balm — first packaging draft

## Recommended title

Fall Asleep at Hogwarts | A Cosy Evening with Neville

The title puts an inviting sleep intent and recognisable setting first, then names the character. It describes the actual experience without making a timed sleep guarantee. This is an editorial recommendation, not a tested audience winner.

## Five title angles

1. Sleep intent: **Fall Asleep at Hogwarts | A Cosy Evening with Neville**
2. Curiosity: **Neville Has One Plant Left… Where Does It Belong? | Sleep Story**
3. Setting: **Hogwarts After Hours | A Rainy Evening & a Gentle Sleep Story**
4. Character: **Neville’s Quiet Hogwarts Evening | Harry Potter Bedtime Story**
5. Cosy escape: **Leave the Day Behind at Hogwarts | Bedtime Story for Adults**

## Thumbnail concepts

1. **HOGWARTS AFTER HOURS** — selected. Warm lantern and a single lemon-balm pot against rain-streaked glass, with Hogwarts in the distance. Large ivory type at left. Familiar world + an inviting quieter side of it. Artwork saved as thumbnail-source.png and export as thumbnail.jpg.
2. **ONE EXTRA POT** — six pots grouped on a bench, with the last pot separated and lit warmly. Creates a gentle question the story actually answers. Pair with the character-first title to avoid explaining the whole premise twice.
3. **YOU CAN REST HERE** — an inviting empty armchair beside a leafy windowsill, blue Hogwarts twilight outside. Emotional escape angle; pair with the setting-first title. This concept is proposed, not generated.

## Description

Ready-to-paste copy is in description.txt. It reflects the final assembly manifest: 27:17 video, synthetic narration, moving visuals, no added music or rain audio. No promise of three-hour runtime, no sleep-in-five-minutes claim and no invented schedule or chapter timestamps.

## Reference-informed rationale

Public metadata snapshots are preserved in video_packaging/research/examples.json. The Sleepy Cat collection references had approximately 2.05M and 978K views; the Get Sleepy bakery reference had approximately 5.61M. They demonstrate examples of recognition + explicit sleep purpose + concrete comforting subject or listening features. They do not prove a causal relationship between a title and performance. Their thumbnail layouts were not audited or copied.

## Accuracy review

- Hogwarts, Neville, Hermione, McGonagall, rain and the extra plant occur in the story.
- The scene is animated; captions are not burned into it.
- Narration is synthetic, not an actor performance or a human narrator credit.
- Rain is visual/story context; the soundtrack has no added rain effects or music.
- The assembled prototype is approximately 27 minutes, not three hours.
- The extra-plant question resolves in the story; the thumbnail contains no threat or false scandal.
