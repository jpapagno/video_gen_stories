At St. Jude's, Mr. Thorne welcomed Dr. Vale into the quiet library. Elara Vance had arrived from Whistlewick at 3:15, carrying the key to Room 42. The old ledger was dated 1842. Beside it lay a sprig of star-anise and a drawing of a night-blooming cereus. Outside, the rain softened, and the lamps glowed along Oak St.
