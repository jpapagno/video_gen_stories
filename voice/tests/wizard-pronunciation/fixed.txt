At Saint Jude's, Mister Thorne welcomed Doctor Vale into the quiet library. Elara Vance had arrived from Whistlewick at quarter past three, carrying the key to Room forty-two. The old ledger was dated eighteen forty-two. Beside it lay a sprig of star-anise and a drawing of a night-blooming cereus. Outside, the rain softened, and the lamps glowed along Oak Street.
