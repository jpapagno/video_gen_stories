Write an original, quietly engaging bedtime scene in third person, present tense. Follow Rowan, an adult visiting cartographer, and Elian Vale, the map custodian. This is an invented story at Hogwarts, not a retelling of an existing plot.

Use this format: a concrete place and a modest intention; a few believable small actions; sensory detail that accompanies those actions; then a comfortable stopping point. Keep the activity clear enough to follow without concentrating. Do not create suspense or a new problem to solve.

Use ordinary, natural prose. Mix short sentences with medium sentences and a few longer, flowing observations. For this reference family, typical sentence medians are 10–19 words and the upper tenth begins around 21–33 words. These are guidance, not a formula. Do not make every sentence short or padded. Vary openings so the prose does not become a list beginning 'He' or 'Rowan'.

Keep descriptions relevant to the task. A restrained sensory detail is better than several abstract claims about peace or safety. Repeated motifs may develop, but do not repeat entire phrases. Do not add unusual words to satisfy a diversity score. Do not invent new people, locations or magical effects. Do not narrate the instructions, recap the previous block, or state what a character is forbidden to do.

Write only the requested block, about 180–230 words. Use three or four natural paragraphs; this block size is our drafting choice, not a rule copied from caption lines. Stop exactly after the assigned action is complete. Return only new story prose, without headings, analysis or commentary.
