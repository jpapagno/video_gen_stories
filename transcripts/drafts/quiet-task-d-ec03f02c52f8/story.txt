He leaves the green-shaded brass lamp burning low to light the room.

Elian steps back slightly, letting the air from the open cabinet circulate into the dry room. The smell of old paper and dust fills the space, mixing with the scent of Rowan's tea. Rowan picks up the faded-blue atlas from its shelf without opening it. His fingers trace the spine, feeling the wear of the binding before he sets it back down gently. Elian watches the movement, his hands resting lightly on the edge of the table.

The small silver ink bottle sits untouched on the dish, waiting for a moment that has not yet come. Rowan looks at the closed atlas, then at the open cabinet door, and finally at the rain against the window. Elian nods slowly, a quiet agreement in the silence between them. The room feels warmer now, filled with the weight of the books rather than the cold of the night. They stand side by side, the task of opening the book done but the work of reading still waiting.

Elian picks up the small silver ink bottle from the brass dish. He places it carefully on the oak worktable, ensuring the lid clicks shut with a soft thud. The metal catches a faint glint from the green-shaded brass lamp hanging above. Rowan shifts his cup of apple tea a few inches to the left, clearing a flat patch of wood. He arranges the brush so the bristles point toward the ink, creating a neat little station beside the bottle. The paper scrap lies flat, ready to receive a single, deliberate stroke if anyone chooses to use it. Rowan watches the arrangement, his eyes tired but content in the dim light. The rain continues its steady drumming against the windowpane, isolating them from the storm outside.

Elian adjusts the lamp slightly, tilting the shade to focus the glow on the paper and ink. The warmth of the bulb contrasts with the cool draft coming from the open cabinet door. Rowan takes a slow sip of his tea, the apple flavor filling his mouth with a comforting sweetness. Elian steps back, letting the tools settle into their final resting place on the table. The atlas remains shut on its shelf, its blue cover untouched by the morning or the night. They stand together in the organized stillness, the work of preparation complete and the room held in gentle balance.
