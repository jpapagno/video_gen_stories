Elian picks up the small silver ink bottle from the brass dish. He places it carefully on the oak worktable, ensuring the lid clicks shut with a soft thud. The metal catches a faint glint from the green-shaded brass lamp hanging above. Rowan shifts his cup of apple tea a few inches to the left, clearing a flat patch of wood. Steam rises slowly from the ceramic, curling upward into the quiet air.

Elian takes the fine-bristle brush and dips it briefly into the ink, wiping the excess on the scrap of paper first. He arranges the brush so the bristles point toward the ink, creating a neat little station beside the bottle. The paper scrap lies flat, ready to receive a single, deliberate stroke if anyone chooses to use it. Rowan watches the arrangement, his eyes tired but content in the dim light. The rain continues its steady drumming against the windowpane, isolating them from the storm outside.

Elian adjusts the lamp slightly, tilting the shade to focus the glow on the paper and ink. The warmth of the bulb contrasts with the cool draft coming from the open cabinet door. Rowan takes a slow sip of his tea, the apple flavor filling his mouth with a comforting sweetness. Elian steps back, letting the tools settle into their final resting place on the table. The atlas remains shut on its shelf, its blue cover untouched by the morning or the night. They stand together in the organized stillness, the work of preparation complete and the room held in gentle balance.
