Rain taps against the high windows while Rowan dips the dry brush into the silver ink bottle, letting a single drop cling to the tip. The paper scrap sits quietly beneath the warm glow of the lamp, waiting for his touch. He moves the bristles carefully, pressing them gently against the white surface to create one thin, ordinary line. Elian watches from the corner of the room and says, "That's enough," as Rowan pauses with the brush still hovering over the mark. The ink sits flat on the page, dark and unmagical under the steady light. Rowan lowers his hand slightly, keeping the open bottle within reach on the wooden table. No spells flare or patterns expand beyond that single stroke of plain silver.
