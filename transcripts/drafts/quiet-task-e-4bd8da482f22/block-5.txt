Rain taps softly against the high, narrow windows as Rowan stands before the worktable, his damp coat still hanging in the entrance hall. He holds the dry brush above the open silver ink bottle, letting the liquid pool slightly at its neck under the steady glow of the green-shaded lamp. The paper scrap waits patiently beneath the light, waiting for a single mark to begin. Rowan touches the bristle tip to the ink with a gentle press, then draws one thin line across the white surface. Elian watches from the side, his hands resting on his hips as he notes the ordinary movement without magic or flourish. He says quietly, "That's enough," acknowledging the simple task completed. The line sits plain and silver on the paper, unchanged by any spell or hidden force. Rowan keeps the brush in hand, the bottle still open, while Elian remains beside the table.
