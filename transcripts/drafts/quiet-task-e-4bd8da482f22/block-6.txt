Rowan sets the brush into the small dish on the tray without looking up. The silver ink sits still inside the bottle, capped now by the stopper Elian had placed there just moments before. He lifts his hand and lets the fine bristles rest in the shallow dish. Elian stands beside him, his old coat collar turned up against the damp air from the hall outside. The green-shaded lamp casts a steady light on the finished silver line across the parchment. Neither of them speaks as they stand there in the quiet dry room. Rowan turns slightly to face Elian, acknowledging the completion of the practice work with a small gesture. They remain at the worktable, the task done and the evening continuing beyond this moment.
