Elian lifts the small tray from the inner shelf and sets it down on the worktable with a soft clink. The silver bottle sits closed among the brushes, its metallic surface catching the light from the green lamp. Rowan watches Elian's steady hands as he arranges the tools, his damp coat still heavy in the hall outside. He does not touch the atlas or lift the cabinet door; it remains shut on the shelves behind them. A faint scent of old parchment mixes with the metallic tang of the ink inside the sealed bottle. Elian places a scrap of paper beside the dish, creating a neat circle of instruments ready for use. The room holds its quiet stillness as Rowan observes the careful placement of every item.
