Rain taps softly against the high glass of the dormitory window, a steady rhythm that fills the dry map room with quiet hum. Elian reaches for the heavy glass ink bottle on the tray, twisting the silver stopper free with a small click that echoes in the stillness. He places the metal cap back down, leaving the viscous liquid exposed to the green-shaded lamp's unblinking glow. Rowan stands close at the worktable, his damp coat still heavy from the entrance hall where he left it hours ago. Elian gestures with a thin finger toward the dark pool of silver fluid on the paper, murmuring only the words 'Only the tip' in a low voice. Rowan lifts the bristles of the old brush by their handle, holding them high above the surface without yet letting them touch the ink. The air smells faintly of dried vellum and the sharp scent of metallic pigment waiting to be stirred.
