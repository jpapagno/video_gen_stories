Rain taps against the high windows as Rowan grips the brass key, its cold metal biting his palm. Elian stands motionless beside him, watching the heavy wooden door of the cabinet remain shut in the dim light. Rowan twists the key with a steady hand, feeling the tumblers click into place before lifting the panel slightly. Elian holds the edge of the door, preventing it from swinging too far open and letting the quiet air settle between them. The green-shaded lamp hums silently on the worktable, casting a soft glow over Rowan's damp coat that still hangs in the entrance hall. He slides the key out of the lock and sets it down on the table near his cup of apple tea. Elian steps back just a fraction, ensuring the cabinet stays ajar but secure while both men remain standing in the room.
