# Editorial rubric and promotion gate

Read samples before looking at prompt labels or local reviewer scores. A good numeric score does not override a distracting passage.

Score each criterion 0–4: 0 fails, 1 major revision, 2 mixed, 3 good with small edits, 4 strong throughout. Include one exact example and a reason for low scores.

| Criterion | Strong writing | Warning signs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read aloud | Natural sentence rhythm; clear references; ordinary dialogue | Tangled syntax, breathless sentences, awkward dialogue tags |
| Specificity | Relevant sensory details accompany believable actions | Adjective lists, generic magic, decorative inventories |
| Gentle movement | Required small actions lead to the scene handoff | Nothing happens, premature episode ending, forced mystery |
| Continuity | Objects, people, viewpoint, time and geography remain consistent | Coat/key teleportation, Elian returns after leaving, winter map changes real weather |
| Sleep tone | Safe and unhurried, with enough substance to follow comfortably | Threats, startling sounds, demands to relax, empty repeated reassurance |
| Freshness | Details develop motifs and vary sentence structure | Repeated lines, repeated endings, recycled metaphors or comfort words |

## First comparison

Two prompts write the arrival scene with the same model digest, seed, target length, outline and continuity. Prompt A is lean; B adds provisional editorial examples. The examples have not been declared user-approved. Compare both without changing other settings to rescue a preferred prompt.

## Promotion

- First select a preferred arrival draft or give a specific revision request.
- Test the candidate prompt on the shared-task, transition and ending fixtures.
- Fix hard word-count/output issues and all confirmed continuity errors.
- Review whether one prompt stays good across these different cases; do not select solely by the mean of a local model's self-reported scores.
- Freeze a prompt only after human review of those cases. Save its version, exact text, model digest and settings.
- Generate production scenes in order. Review each before updating authoritative continuity.
- At chapter boundaries, inspect changes in character knowledge, object locations, resolved tasks, repeated imagery and novelty level.
- When complete, assemble the accepted transcript, check the real word count and synthesize a small calibration passage before budgeting the final audio. The three-hour requirement is checked against actual audio duration.

No authoring prompt is guaranteed to produce good prose every time. This process makes failures observable and revisions traceable rather than treating a seed or rubric score as a quality guarantee.
