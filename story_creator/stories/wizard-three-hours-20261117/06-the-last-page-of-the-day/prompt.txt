Write a complete, original wizarding bedtime story for adults, approximately 4,600 words long, intended for about 33 minutes of gentle narration.

Set it in the Harry Potter universe, using established characters such as Harry Potter, Hermione Granger, Ron Weasley, Neville Longbottom, Luna Lovegood, Professor McGonagall or Hagrid. Choose a small cast and keep their personalities, relationships and dialogue recognisable. Use familiar locations such as Hogwarts or Hogsmeade and everyday enchantments. Choose a peaceful period that fits the characters' ages and circumstances, and keep the timeline consistent. Use third-person narration and British English.

Give the main character a modest but meaningful purpose. Let the story unfold through connected events, gentle discoveries and warm interactions, with a satisfying resolution. Keep the stakes low and gradually settle the pace towards a peaceful ending.

Build the story around one gentle question or unfinished task introduced early, let discoveries across the scenes gradually resolve it, and end with the character settling to sleep.

Use natural, varied prose that sounds good aloud. Include sensory details where they enrich the scene, while keeping the story moving. Avoid danger, combat, frightening imagery, repetitive descriptions and padding.

Make the speaker of every dialogue turn clear to someone listening without seeing the text. Use a brief attribution, such as “Mara asked” or “Elian replied,” or an immediately adjacent action that names the speaker. Do not rely on alternating lines alone to identify speakers, especially for short questions and answers. Keep these attributions natural and unobtrusive.

Choose the cast from the established characters and invent an entirely new plot. Do not retell events from the books or films, reproduce their dialogue, or imitate J. K. Rowling's prose style. Return only the title and complete story, without commentary or an outline.
