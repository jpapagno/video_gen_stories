The existing recording is `outputs/voice/last-pot-of-lemon-balm-wizard.wav`, with a measured duration of 1,636.92 seconds (27 minutes 16.92 seconds). Its spoken text exactly matches the original prepared transcript after whitespace normalisation. The 3,815 prepared words give a measured pace of approximately 139.84 words per minute. Three hours therefore needs approximately 25,170 words at that pace.

`base-prompt.txt` preserves the saved prompt exactly. New story prompts retain its setting, third-person British English, low stakes, clear dialogue attribution and sleeping ending. The only adjustments are a longer word target and a different quiet task for each story. The writer uses the existing GPT Sol 6.1 / low (Light) generation workflow.

Each story folder contains the exact prompt, untouched raw prose, a separate pronunciation-prepared transcript, and a generation receipt. `combined-transcript.txt` and `collection.json` are produced after every story finishes successfully. Story titles are included, as in the original narration.

The runtime in the manifest is an estimate based on the existing voice recording. New narration and a new three-hour video have not been rendered. The final recording must be measured before claiming an exact three-hour duration.
