The useful synthesis is searchable setting + bedtime purpose + a specific comforting hook. For this episode, Hogwarts/Neville supplies recognition; an extra pot seeking a window supplies gentle curiosity; the greenhouse supplies a cosy visual. Lead with the world viewers recognise rather than only the internal literary title.

Borrow the structure, not exact copy or branded artwork. Only use features our final file actually contains. Visible rain is not rain audio. Our roughly 27-minute prototype is not a multi-hour collection. Sleep benefits are an intended listening use, not a guaranteed outcome. Suggested editorial preferences such as 45–80-character titles and short thumbnail text are hypotheses for testing, not thresholds derived from the references.

Thumbnail concepts in this module are our design proposals. Competitor thumbnail images were not visually audited, so no claim about their specific layouts is made.
